# 42 Piscine

42 is an innovative coding school built around a different approach to learning: no traditional lectures, no teachers standing in front of a classroom, and no predefined lessons to follow. Instead, students learn by solving problems, experimenting, breaking things, and figuring out how to put them back together.

The Piscine is an intensive selection process where you dive into programming from scratch, working through a series of individual and group projects. The methodology is based on peer-to-peer learning: you are expected to learn how to learn, help others when you can, and ask for help when you can't.

Projects are evaluated both by fellow students and by **Moulinette**, an automated grading system that is considerably less forgiving than your average human being.
